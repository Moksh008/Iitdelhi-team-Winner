# Product and scope

Satark helps a person pause before sending money after an urgent accident,
hospital, police, or family-emergency claim. It extracts explainable signals
from consented text and presents independent verification options.

In scope: multilingual transcript input, emergency/payment indicators,
named-location lookup, nearby public listings, user-initiated dialer actions,
and a compact overlay control. Out of scope: automatic payments, automatic
calling, caller identity proof, and unrestricted cellular-call recording.

Safety principle: a risk signal is not a verdict. Missing or unfamiliar
listings do not prove that a claim is false.
